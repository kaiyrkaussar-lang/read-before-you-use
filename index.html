🧠 4-Step Method

Use this whenever you meet an unfamiliar instruction.

READ → FIND → CHECK → ACT
1

READ

Read the whole sentence. Don't stop at the first unfamiliar word.

2

FIND

Find the main action.

Ask: What should I do?

3

CHECK

Look for important signals:

🚨 DO NOT · DON'T · NEVER · AVOID

⏰ BEFORE · AFTER

📦 STORE · KEEP

4

ACT

Only after you understand the instruction should you follow it.

Example

Charge the device before first use.

First: charge
Then: use
