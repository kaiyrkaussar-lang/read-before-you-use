Read Before You Use

A practical English guide for understanding product instructions in everyday life.

Read. Find. Check. Act.

Why do we need this guide?

English is not only a school subject. We can meet English instructions on cosmetics, electronic devices, chargers and many other products.

You may know individual English words but still have difficulties understanding what you actually need to do.

Remember: You do not always need to understand every word. You need to understand the important information.

🧠 The READ → FIND → CHECK → ACT strategy

This guide teaches you not only vocabulary, but also a strategy for reading instructions.

1. READ Read the whole instruction.
2. FIND Find the main action and key words.
3. CHECK Check warnings, conditions and time.
4. ACT Do what the instruction tells you.
Example:

"Unplug the charger before cleaning the device."

READ: Read the complete sentence.
FIND: Main action = unplug.
CHECK: Before cleaning.
ACT: Disconnect the charger first.

🧴 English in everyday situations

🧴

Cosmetics

Instructions may tell you how to apply a product, how long to leave it and what to avoid.

Example:
"Apply to clean skin. Avoid contact with eyes. Rinse thoroughly after use."
🔌

Chargers

Electronic products often contain warnings and instructions about electricity.

Example:
"Unplug the charger before cleaning. Do not use near water."
💻

Electronics

Instructions can contain actions, conditions and warnings.

Example:
"Keep the device away from heat. Do not cover the ventilation openings."
🧼

Household products

Pay attention to prohibitions and safety information.

Example:
"Keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow."
